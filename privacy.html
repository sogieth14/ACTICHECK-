2. Information we handle

A. Account information that we hold

When you create an account we collect:

  • your email address, and whether it has been verified;
  • how you sign in (email, or Google). If you use Google, we receive the name, email address and profile picture that Google shares, and we keep only what we need;
  • security details, such as sign-in times, a record of failed attempts, and your two-step verification settings. Passwords are never stored in a readable form;
  • a record that you accepted this policy and the Terms, with the version and date.

B. Clinical information that stays on your device

When you use ActiCheck you may enter file names, ages, symptoms, vitals, exam findings, test results, photos and notes. This information is stored only on your device. We do not collect it, we do not have a copy, and our servers cannot read it. Health information is "sensitive personal data" under the NDPA, which is why we keep it off our servers.

Please use initials, a nickname or a bed number for file names, not full names.

C. Information you choose to share

A QR code, link or full file that you create contains the case information. If you send it to someone, for example by WhatsApp, the app you use and the person who receives it can read it. You can lock a code or file with a PIN, but a short PIN gives only light protection.

D. Technical information

  • Our hosting provider and the sign-in provider keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time of request, to run and protect the service.
  • The app currently loads fonts from Google. This sends your IP address to Google when the font is first downloaded. [CONFIRM: remove this line once fonts are hosted by us]

E. What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, or patient records. The camera is used on your device only to scan QR codes and to take photos you choose to attach, and these images are not sent to us.

3. How we use it

  • to create and run your account, and to check that it is you signing in;
  • to send verification, security and important service messages;
  • to prevent abuse, such as repeated password guessing, fake sign-ups and automated attacks;
  • to deliver app updates and updates to the clinical rules, and to give you support;
  • to meet legal duties, and to defend legal claims.

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. We will ask for your consent before sending any marketing email or adding any analytics.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account information: while your account is open. If you delete your account, we remove it within [CONFIRM: 30] days, and from backups within [CONFIRM: 90] days, unless the law requires us to keep something.
  • Security logs: up to [CONFIRM: 12] months.
  • Information on your device: until you delete it. Deleting your account does not remove files on your device. Signing out does not remove them either. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes them for good, and we cannot recover them, so export anything you need first.

8. Security

We protect the service with encrypted connections, sign-in lockouts and rate limits on repeated attempts, checks that requests come from the real app, strict controls on what the app is allowed to load, validation of every number and file the app receives, and a rule that secret keys are never placed in the app itself. Patient information is kept off our servers.

No system is completely secure, and your own care matters too. Use a phone lock, keep your device and browser up to date, and do not share your account.

If a personal data breach is likely to put people at risk, we will tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and we will tell affected people as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold and how we use it;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information, where the law allows;
  • restrict, or object to, certain uses of your information;
  • give you your information in a portable format;
  • stop relying on your consent, if consent is the basis.

You can delete your account yourself in the app, under Account and security. For anything else, contact us using the details in section 14. We may need to confirm who you are first, and we aim to reply within [CONFIRM: 30] days. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

10. If you are a clinician using patient information

Because patient information stays on your device, you or your hospital or clinic decide why and how it is used. You are responsible for following your professional duties, your institution's rules, and the law on patient confidentiality and consent.

  • Enter only what you need. Prefer initials, nicknames or bed numbers over full names.
  • Think before you share. A QR code, link, card image or full file carries the case with it. Use a PIN, share with the right person, and delete messages you no longer need.
  • Protect your phone with a lock code, and report a lost or stolen device to your institution.
  • Photos of test results may contain patient names or numbers. Check before you share them.

We cannot see, recover, or delete patient information on your device, because we do not have it.

11. Children

ActiCheck is for healthcare professionals aged 18 or over. It is not meant for children. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it. Clinicians who record information about child patients must handle it with the extra care the law requires.

12. Storage on your device

ActiCheck uses your browser's local storage, IndexedDB and a service worker cache. These keep your files, photos, settings (such as light or dark mode) and the app itself on your device so that it works offline. They are essential to how the app works. They are not used for advertising or tracking. If we ever add analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
